002 / lines

A lattice that follows your hand.

A thousand points on a sphere, drawn as wire and projected by hand. It turns on its own, leans toward the pointer, and keeps spinning after a flick.

drag to spin
003 / goo

Separate things, one surface.

Orbs drift and merge where they meet. The seam is not drawn: a blur and a contrast curve find it. Move your pointer in and the field reaches for it.

pointer adds an orb
004 / dock

Proximity is the whole interaction.

No hover states, no borders lighting up. Each tile measures its distance to the pointer and grows by a curve, so the row behaves like one soft object.

005 / stack

Depth you can feel.

Push a card and the stack pays attention: the oldest bows out, the rest settle into new positions on a spring. Hover to fan the pile.

006 / orbit

A carousel with weight.

Eight cards on a ring, each turned to face you. Drag and let go: momentum carries it, then friction takes over. Every card stays readable at every angle.
